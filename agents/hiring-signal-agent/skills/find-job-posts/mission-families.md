# Mission families

> A list that grows. It starts empty for your offer: the agent fills it with you at your first campaign, from your
> offer (`context/offer.md`) and 5 real posts. Each job post read after that adds to it: a new wording for a family, or
> a new family when a post does not fit any. The human validates before the file changes.

A job post lists missions. A family groups the missions that call for the same offer from you. The agent reads this
file before scoring a post.

## The families

| Family | The missions it groups | Words seen in real posts | What we offer | Seen in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 · <name> | <missions> | <wordings read in posts> | <one sentence: what your offer does for these missions> | <n> posts |

Example from the campaigns this pack was built on (an outreach platform): "F1 · Outbound by signals: outbound,
sequences, a pipeline engine triggered by intent. Words seen: signal-based pipeline engine, outbound triggered by real
intent. What we offer: the agent finds the leads and prepares the campaign."

A post can belong to several families. For a message, take the first that applies, in the order of the table.

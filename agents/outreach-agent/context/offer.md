# Your offer

The agent reads this file before it searches, sorts or writes anything. On the first run it fills it with you: the 6
questions of "Must" first, in one message, then the rest only if you want. Replace every `<...>`.
`offer.example.md`, next to this file, shows it filled for a real company: read it for the level of detail, never copy
it.

## Must (the agent asks these on the first run)

1. **What you sell, in one line**: <what the product does, for whom, in the buyer's words>
2. **What you do not do**: <what people often expect from you and you do not offer, so the agent never claims it>
3. **Who you sell to**: <roles that buy, roles that use it, industries, company sizes, countries>
4. **Who to leave out**: <competitors, customers, partners, your own team, segments you do not serve>
5. **Language and sender**: <the language of the messages (or "the language of each lead"), the name and role of the person who signs>
6. **What the last message offers**: <the low-friction next step you send: a trial, a demo video, a template, a short audit, a call>

## Nice to have

- Proof you can quote: <customers, numbers, reviews you are allowed to cite, with where they are public>
- Claims you never make: <words, promises or comparisons you do not want>
- Integrations you have: <so the agent only names those>
- The call to action you prefer in the first message: <a reply to a question, a resource, a call>
- Tone: <for example direct, no jargon, no exclamation marks>

## Your brand (only for pages and visuals)

- Colors: <main color, accent color, as hex codes>
- Font: <the font of your site>
- Logo: <a link to your logo>

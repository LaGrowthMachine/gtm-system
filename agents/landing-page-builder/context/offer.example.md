# Your offer · EXAMPLE

> An example, filled with the public offer of La Growth Machine (the company that maintains these agents), to show
> the level of detail the agent needs. It is not your offer: your own `offer.md` is what the agent reads.

## Must

1. **What you sell, in one line**: a multichannel outreach platform (LinkedIn, email, voice) that runs the campaigns,
   the conversations and the follow-up, and that you can drive from Claude or ChatGPT through its MCP.
2. **What you do not do**: no lead database and no list building of its own (it connects to Clay, Apollo, Cognism);
   not a CRM (it syncs with HubSpot and Pipedrive); no promise of a reply rate.
3. **Who you sell to**: heads of sales, founders and growth or RevOps leads of B2B companies of 10 to 200 people,
   mostly SaaS, agencies and B2B services, in Europe and North America.
4. **Who to leave out**: other outreach tools (lemlist, HeyReach, Waalaxy and the like), current customers, partners,
   our own team, consumer businesses.
5. **Language and sender**: the language of each lead (English or French); the sender is the person who owns the
   campaign, first name only.
6. **What the last message offers**: the agent to test, one skill to add to Claude or ChatGPT and the MCP to connect
   it, with a free trial.

## Nice to have

- Proof you can quote: the ratings shown on G2 and Capterra, customer names already public on the website.
- Claims you never make: a number of meetings, "replace your SDRs", any price in a cold message.
- Integrations you have: HubSpot and Pipedrive (sync), Clay (two-way), the API and the MCP for the rest.
- The call to action you prefer in the first message: one question about the lead's situation.
- Tone: direct, positive words, no jargon.
